## Icon generation

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final square application icon for MeshMatsu, a Meshtastic messaging app running on the Tanmatsu handheld.
Primary request: an original, confident, exceptionally simple geometric logo: a bold capital M constructed from thick connected mesh links and five solid circular network nodes, enclosed by a subtle wide handheld-screen outline. The M must be instantly readable at small launcher-icon sizes.
Composition: one centered icon only, large symbol filling a charcoal rounded-square tile with generous balanced margins. Outside the rounded tile is genuinely transparent. The inner screen frame is a simple landscape rounded rectangle, wider than tall; no detailed device drawing. The M is the hero, with five nodes following its five structural vertices; no additional spiderweb or decorative connections.
Style: crisp flat graphic, precise consistent strokes, restrained retro handheld/terminal character, clean silhouette, polished app identity, vector-friendly geometry.
Color palette: mint green #67EA94 symbol and screen outline, deep charcoal #101513 tile. Two flat colors only with antialiased edges.
Text: no wordmark, no letters other than the M-shaped symbol, no captions.
Avoid: gradients, glow, shadows, 3D, mockups, thin fiddly detail, extra wireless arcs, existing Meshtastic logos, keyboards, watermarks, presentation boards, multiple options.
